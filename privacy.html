Privacy Policy

Effective 12 November 2026

This policy explains what the Sankara Catalog app (for iPhone, iPad, Android, Mac and Windows) does with information. The app is made by Sankara Furniture Design (“Sankara”, “we”). It lets Sankara’s sales team and trade partners browse Sankara’s furniture catalog, including when the device is offline.

The short version

  • The app has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We do not sell or share your information for advertising.
  • The only personal information the app handles is your catalog account: the email and password you log in with, and the account details our catalog server sends back.
  • Catalog photos, PDFs and product details are saved on your device so they work offline. Logging out removes them, along with your account details.

Your account

Sankara creates accounts for its team and partners. You cannot sign up in the app. When you log in, the app sends your email and password over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to Sankara’s catalog server at catalog.sankarafurnituredesign.com, which checks them.

The server then sends back your account details: name, email, phone number (if one is on your account), role and company. The app keeps these on your device to show who is logged in and which sections of the catalog your role can see.

The app does not store your password. To keep you logged in, it keeps the login session the server gives it, the same way a web browser keeps you logged in to a website.

Catalog content saved on your device

To work offline, the app saves product details, photos, presentations and PDFs in its own private storage on your device. Some of this content comes packaged with the app. The rest is downloaded from Sankara’s servers and from the content-delivery service that hosts Sankara’s catalog files (Amazon CloudFront).

When your device is online, the app checks Sankara’s catalog server for new or changed products and saves them. On iPhone, iPad and Android, the operating system may also run this check in the background while the app is closed. These requests only fetch catalog content for your account. They send nothing else about you or your device.

Permissions

  • Add to Photos (iPhone and iPad): used only when you choose to save a product photo. The app can add photos to your library but cannot read it.
  • Sharing: when you share a photo, PDF or link, your device’s share sheet sends it to the app or person you pick. The app does not see what happens after that.
  • Network: the app checks whether you are on Wi-Fi or mobile data so it can avoid large downloads on mobile data.

The app does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or health data.

What we do not do

  • No advertising or advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software from other companies.
  • No tracking of you across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • No selling or renting of personal information.

Where your account is kept

Your account and the catalog are held on Sankara’s catalog service. Sankara uses them only to run the catalog for its team and partners.

Removing your information

  • From your device: use Log out on the app’s Sync screen. This removes the saved catalog, photos, PDFs and your account details from the device. Deleting the app removes everything as well.
  • Your account: to have your catalog account and its information deleted, email us at the address below. We will confirm when it is done.

Security

All communication between the app and Sankara’s servers uses HTTPS. Content saved by the app sits in the app’s private storage, which other apps cannot read.

Children

The app is a business tool for Sankara’s team and trade partners. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
