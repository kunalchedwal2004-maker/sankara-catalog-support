Support

Sankara Catalog puts Sankara Furniture Design’s full furniture catalog on your phone, tablet or computer: products, photos, sets, presentations and the company profile. It all keeps working offline.

Common questions

How do I get an account?

Accounts are given by Sankara Furniture Design to its sales team and trade partners. If you work with Sankara and need access, email us and we will set one up for you. You can then log in with the same email and password as the online catalog.

Does the app work without internet?

Yes. The catalog comes with the app, and everything else is saved on your device after you log in. You can browse products, photos and presentations, and open them full screen, with no connection.

How do new products reach the app?

Automatically. Whenever your device is online, the app checks for new or changed products and saves them. On iPhone, iPad and Android this can also happen while the app is closed. To check straight away, open the app’s Sync screen.

Can I avoid using mobile data?

The company videos are only downloaded on Wi-Fi. On the Sync screen you can also choose the photo quality, which decides how much data and space the photos use.

How do I log out or remove the saved catalog?

Open the Sync screen and tap Log out. This removes the saved catalog and your account details from the device.

How do I delete my account?

Email us from the address on your account and ask for it to be deleted. We will delete your catalog account and confirm when it is done.
